What is RAG

RAG - retrieval-augmented generation, adds a search step before a language model answers a question. The search first finds the parts of documents that best match the question, and the model then writes its answer from those parts instead of relying on its memory alone.

GraphRAG, or graph-based RAG, goes one step further and builds a graph of connected entities, mentioned in the documents, together with the links between them. It can then find information by following these links.
